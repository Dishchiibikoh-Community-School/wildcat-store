Leader in Me 7 Habits

1. Be Proactive

Take responsibility for your actions and focus on what you can control.

Examples: Speak up in class, ask for help, complete assignments on time, make good choices.

2. Begin with the End in Mind

Set goals, define success, and create a plan to achieve it.

Examples: Plan your week, study for tests ahead of time, set academic goals, visualize success.

3. Put First Things First

Prioritize important tasks, manage time wisely, and focus on goals.

Examples: Do homework before video games, organize your time, focus during class, say no to distractions.

4. Think Win-Win

Approach conflicts looking for solutions that benefit everyone.

Examples: Share resources with classmates, find compromises, celebrate others' success, help a friend succeed.

5. Seek First to Understand, Then to Be Understood

Practice empathetic listening to understand others' perspectives.

Examples: Listen without interrupting, ask questions, see things from others' viewpoint, be respectful.

6. Synergize

Work together to achieve more through collaboration and valuing differences.

Examples: Join group projects, respect different opinions, work as a team, lean on others' strengths.

7. Sharpen the Saw

Take care of your body, mind, and spirit through regular renewal and balance.

Examples: Exercise, read, get good sleep, eat healthy, meditate, take breaks to recharge.
